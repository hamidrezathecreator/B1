# Level 1 pickup and placement

Open `Scenes/Production/Lvl_1.unity` and select **Lvl1 Build Level State Machine**.
Its **Tutorial** reference points to the scene's **Lvl1 Tutorial** component on the same object.

## Helicopter start

The player starts under **Helicoopter > XRParent** with half the normal eye and body height. Move **XRParent** to set the cabin seat; headset rotation and controller interaction retain their normal tracking. All locomotion providers (walking, teleport, turning, grab movement, climbing, jumping and gravity) are disabled and the rig stays pinned to the seat. Physical headset translation in all three axes is cancelled through a shared tracking-space offset, so room-scale walking, leaning and crouching cannot move the viewpoint outside the cabin. The offset applies to camera and controllers together, including after the headset's before-render tracking update.

**Mission Start** asks **Lvl1 Build Level State Machine** to run **Fade Out + Transfer To Tutorial + Fade In**. It fades the headset view and cabin audio out, waits for full black, then returns the player to **_XR > Mission Start Bridge Spawn** at the original tutorial position and facing. Normal eye/body height is restored while black. The player stays locked through the black hold and fade-in; walking and the existing move/pickup/placement tutorial start only when the view is fully visible. Repeated presses are ignored. **Helicopter Fade Out Seconds**, **Helicopter Black Seconds** and **Helicopter Fade In Seconds** are on the state machine and run in real time, including when game time is paused.

The state machine now displays **Helicopter Start**, **Fade Out + Transfer To Tutorial + Fade In**, **Bridge Tutorial**, then the existing missile, fall and level-load states. Inspector jump buttons use the same state names; debug jumps to later states explicitly complete the opening and release the player first.

At full black, the player detaches before the entire helicopter GameObject is disabled. This stops its hover and hides the cabin and Mission Start UI. Movement remains locked until fade-in finishes. Debug jumps past the opening also disable the helicopter.

Room-scale compensation remains active through the complete fade-out. It resets only at full black, and the tutorial arrival aligns the current headset position and facing with the spawn marker, accounting for any real-world walking during the ride. Normal physical tracking resumes on the bridge; stick locomotion stays locked until fade-in finishes.

**Cabin Audio** on **Helicopter Hover** references separate **Helicopter SFX** and **Helicopter Music** children. Their clip, volume and pitch controls are shown directly in the hover Inspector. Assign your own clips; empty slots stay silent. Both sources loop during the ride, fade out with the view, and stop at the transfer. They do not replace bridge ambience, dialogue or later music.

Select **Helicoopter > Helicopter Hover** to tune flight. **Hover Center** references the existing **HelicopterHoverCenter**. **Height Above Center** is the flight altitude relative to that point (initially preserving the authored 220.3-metre helicopter height). **Use Starting Radius** preserves the authored horizontal distance; turn it off to set **Radius**. **Orbit Seconds** and **Clockwise** control circling. **Sideways Yaw** -90 aims the right side at the center; 90 aims the left side. Scene gizmos show the cyan orbit, direction arrows, center marker, labelled altitude/radius, yellow seat/eye height, green tutorial arrival/facing and a dotted transfer line. Drag the vertical altitude handle or, with **Use Starting Radius** off, the radius handle to author the route with Undo support.

**Preset** offers **Calm**, **Normal**, **Rough** and **Custom**. Changing a preset applies vibration and turbulence settings once; each field can then be tuned independently. **Normal Vibration** controls continual position/rotation shake and frequency. **Air Turbulence** adds slower wind and smooth gusts with separate strength, frequency, intensity, duration and random quiet intervals. Both motion layers can be disabled independently. The component menu also has **Trigger Turbulence (Play Mode)** and preset reset actions. A single PrimeTween driver combines the orbit and both motion layers without competing transform tweens. Flight settings remain editable during Play Mode; restart hover to recapture the starting radius or change its time mode.

## Pickup and placement

- **Pickup Cases** assigns the objects that advance the tutorial. Each needs an authored Rigidbody, collider and XR Grab Interactable. The current two cases already have these components. The first case's saved hand poses are preserved.
- **Placement Zones** assigns the scene's **Tutorial Placement A** and **Tutorial Placement B** objects. Move or rotate either object to choose the final case pose. Its yellow preview is a child and follows it in the editor.
- Each zone's **Accepted Object** or **Accepted Objects** controls which pickups it accepts. Set **Snap Radius**, **Pull In Radius** and **Snap Seconds** on the zone. An unassigned zone accepts nothing.
- **Hide When Filled** assigns the optional preview to hide on placement. Keep that preview separate from the zone's GameObject so hiding it does not stop the snap coroutine.
- **Scene References** assigns the player origin, Start button, both controllers, font and teleport rays directly. The movement banner and thumbstick highlight use the left controller; the Start hint uses the right controller. All hand hints use RTLTMPro with the assigned Persian font (Peyda Bold in the production scene). Object names do not affect pickup or placement.
- Teleport is disabled on both controllers and the teleportation provider in the XR Origin prefab. The tutorial keeps teleport off and advances the movement instruction after walking 0.4 meters from its starting position.
- Level 1 opening dialogue begins its configured 10-second delay after the helicopter transfer and fade-in finish. Other levels retain their scene-start dialogue timing.

Normal progression: Start → move → switch controller visuals to hands → pick up an assigned case → place it in an assigned zone → finish snapping → completion message → first missile and explosion → remaining level sequence. The hands appear when the grab instruction begins, with a controller vibration. Missing assignments block completion and report an error. Picking up or dropping a case elsewhere cannot start the explosions.

The state machine Inspector has a button to select the tutorial setup. Explosion shortcuts are explicit Play Mode debugging overrides; advancing the tutorial itself cannot bypass placement.

Levels 2 and 3 always use the project VR hands with controller visuals hidden, including when either level is opened directly. Level 1 starts with controllers until the grab instruction begins.

After placement, a fighter jet pass precedes the first missile. The first explosion and cloud form at full speed for 0.5 seconds, followed by a two-second slow-motion recovery. Cloud expansion and debris travel follow global slow motion. A separate pressure front compares distance traveled against explosion-to-headset distance and immediately triggers shake, CentralScope, vibration and **Objects To Throw** when it arrives, including during slow motion. Feedback works even if the cloud is hidden, destroyed or skipped by the pool. **Player Listener** points to the tracked camera; assigning an XR Origin or one of its children also resolves to its camera. Thrown mesh centers fly at a random point within **Throw Aim Radius** of the headset instead of homing into the same spot, and distance-based travel timing triggers their feedback on arrival without collisions. **Attack Fraction** sets cloud expansion time as a fraction of its lifetime (Level 1: 2.5 scaled seconds). **Throw Speed**, **Maximum Throw Seconds**, **Throw Spacing**, **Player Hit Radius** and **Throw Aim Radius** tune the barrage.

The second missile starts when the recovery finishes. The fall continuously shakes through PrimeTween and turns toward random headings biased toward the ground; **Pitch Range** is 60–85 degrees down in the scene. **Rotation Degrees Per Second** controls the turn rate. Ground contact stops descent and wind, then plays the stronger **Ground Impact** position/rotation shake and controller pulse. Tune its strength, duration and frequency on **XR Shock Fall Sequence**. Late debris cannot replace the landing shake. **Maximum Fall Seconds** bounds the wait before transition when no ground is available. Missile whoosh and fighter jet clips can be assigned in the Inspector; empty assignments use generated audio.

Only the first cloud pressure front reaching the headset starts **Objects To Throw**. Later clouds keep their visual and pressure feedback. The resolved headset is cached for pressure tracking.

The second impact immediately stops the first bridge's scatter tweens and disables all its pieces, including merge drivers and followers. **Second Strike Scatter Delay Seconds** then waits 0.5 real seconds before the second bridge and object scatters start. **Fall After Second Strike Seconds** waits another 0.15 seconds after scatter starts before falling. These delays belong to the state machine, so cancelling the sequence cancels pending scatter work. **Reset Explosion** restores the disabled pieces' previous active states.

Fall fog uses runtime exponential fog. Graphics Settings keeps all three fog modes explicitly so builds retain the runtime mode even when authored scenes use Exp2. **Target Fog Density**, **Fog Ramp Seconds**, **Fog Curve Exponent** and **Fog Color** remain editable on the fall sequence.

On each **Explosion Scatter**, populate **Disable On Scatter** with mesh GameObjects to hide when a full or partial scatter starts. **Reset Explosion** restores their original active states, including objects that were already inactive. The editor scatter preview supports the same hide/reset behavior.

CentralScope is off during idle gameplay. Edit `Assets/Scripts/CentralScope/CentralScope/CentralScopeSettings.asset` to tune its separate **Shake** (subtle) and **Fall** (extreme) blur/vignette presets. Shake pulses fade out automatically; falling holds the Fall preset until landing or cancellation. Android and Standalone renderers share this settings asset.
